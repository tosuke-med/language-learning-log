title: What I feel about good scientific presentations
date: 2026-05-09
language: en
tags: [speaking]
---
Many textbooks say that, in scientific presentations, if you want to speak logically, you need to use conjunctions effectively, or you need to use transitional phrases like “first” or “next” or something like that. But I feel that that is not the important point for building a good scientific presentation. Because when I watch lectures by researchers, even when I see a good presentation, they don't use a lot of conjunctions in their presentation. But in good presentations, they are very good at using the flow, or I should say, the story is good. They gradually ask us to imagine the situation, share the emotion, and ask the question. But just after that, they answer that question. So this process, it’s kind of a shared cognitive process, is an important factor for a good presentation. It’s very useful for lectures, but it’s also useful for research presentations, because if we want to understand the results or interpretation of the results, we need to follow the cognitive process of the researchers.
